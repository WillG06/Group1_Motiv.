# CS2TP Group 1 Car Rental Web App (Motiv)
---
## **Overview**

Motiv is a full stack car rental web application designed to provide a fast, simple and hassle free booking experience, particularly for people travelling across the UK. The platform allows users to browse vehicles, make bookings and manage rentals efficiently, while administrators manage vehicle stock and reservations.

The aim is to remove the typical difficulties associated with car renting by offering a clear and user friendly system with a smooth step by step booking process.

---

## **Key Features**

* Car listings with detailed information
* Search and sorting functionality
* Favourites option + a gamified reward system
* Booking system with availability management
* A customer chatbot + customer inquiry section
* Payment system integration
* User registration and login system
* Admin dashboard for managing vehicles, bookings and queries
* Stock management system
* Accessibility focused design

---

## **Target Audience**

* Tourists visiting major UK cities
* Travellers needing quick and reliable transport
* Young drivers seeking affordable rental options

---

## **Technology Stack**

**Frontend**
HTML, CSS, JavaScript

**Backend**
PHP

**Database**
MySQL

These technologies were selected due to their reliability, efficiency and strong support, allowing the development of a responsive and scalable application.

---

## **Project Aim**

The goal of Motiv is to create a straightforward and efficient car rental platform that enables users to quickly book vehicles with minimal effort. The system is designed to support users on tight schedules, including those arriving at airports, by providing a smooth transition from arrival to vehicle collection.
